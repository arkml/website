Arjun Maheswaran

I'm the co-founder and CEO of Rowboat Labs (YC S24).

We build Rowboat, an open-source team of AI coworkers. It has 18,000+ GitHub stars, in the top 0.01% of projects on GitHub.

I believe AI agents will soon outnumber the people in most companies. Rowboat is the infrastructure for them to collaborate safely, with humans in the control seat.

I've been shipping machine learning to production since 2012, from Twitter's first realtime spam models to training LLMs with reinforcement learning in 2020. I previously co-founded Agara AI, which Coinbase acquired. I hold 7 AI patents.
