Arjun Maheswaran

Co-founder and CEO, Rowboat Labs (YC S24)

I'm the co-founder and CEO of Rowboat Labs. We build Rowboat, an open-source, local-first AI coworker that builds a knowledge graph of your work and acts on it. It has 18,000+ GitHub stars, in the top 0.01% of projects on GitHub.

I've been shipping machine learning to production since 2012. I built Twitter's first realtime spam detection and deep learning models, co-founded Agara AI, which Coinbase acquired, and later led deep learning efforts at Coinbase. I hold 7 AI patents, including early work on training language models with reinforcement learning.
